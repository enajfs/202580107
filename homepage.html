Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering your favorite treats.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend placing your order at least 3 days in advance, especially for custom cakes and special events.

Do you deliver outside Puerto Princesa?

Currently, we offer delivery within Puerto Princesa City only.

Can I customize my cake design?

Yes! You can request a custom cake design when placing your order. Please provide your preferred design and details in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery and available online payment options. Please check the payment options at checkout for details.

Can I cancel or reschedule my order?

Cancellation and rescheduling requests are subject to our Cancellation Policy. Please review the policy or contact us for assistance.
